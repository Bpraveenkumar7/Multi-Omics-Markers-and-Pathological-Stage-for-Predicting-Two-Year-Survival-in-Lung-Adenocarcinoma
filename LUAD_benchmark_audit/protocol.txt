Primary experiment: train on all 267 TCGA patients after TCGA-only model selection. Test once on the 85 paper-listed CPTAC-3 patients. Outcome is death by the study's 2-year definition from Table S1. Use only raw assay-derived measurements for predictors plus baseline pathological stage (I to IV, ordered). Use GDC harmonized methylation beta and RNA log2(TPM+1) for both cohorts (450K/EPIC). Use 20 CpGs and 12 RNA features from the published shared panel. Comparator: molecular random forest. Candidate: ridge logistic regression with the molecular panel and stage. Ablations: stage-only and molecular-only ridge. Use training medians and training scales. Tune ridge C over [0.01,0.1,1,10] and RF max_features over ['sqrt',0.5,1.0] in stratified 5-fold training validation. RF uses 300 trees and class_weight='balanced_subsample'. Retain all training patients. Same outer folds, seed 20261125. Primary outcomes are external ROC AUC for each model and candidate minus RF, with 95% paired event-stratified patient bootstrap interval for the difference. Also report average precision and Brier scores. No CPTAC tuning or calibration in this primary analysis. Secondary: within-cohort 10-fold CV with 5-fold inner model tuning for 32-feature RF and ridge+stage; also TCGA 40-feature RF, 32-feature molecular ridge, and stage alone. Label comparisons conditional on authors' TCGA-selected panel, approximate benchmark only. Descriptive sensitivity: predictions on all 96 originally eligible CPTAC patients, including the 11 paper exclusions, using the same locked models and imputers. No refitting based on the external scores.
